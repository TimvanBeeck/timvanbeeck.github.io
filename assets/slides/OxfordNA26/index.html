Motivation

Trefftz methods in a nutshell

Solve

\[ -\Delta u = 0 \ \text{in } \Omega, \qquad u = g \ \text{on } \partial\Omega \]

Ritz–Galerkin Ritz (1909) Galerkin (1915)

trial functions satisfy the boundary condition \[ u_h|_{\partial\Omega} = g \]

solve \[ \int_\Omega \nabla u_h \cdot \nabla v_h \,dx = 0 \quad \forall v_h \in V_h \cap H^1_0(\Omega) \]

$\tfrac{(p+1)(p+2)}{2} = \mathcal{O}(p^d)$ dofs per element

Trefftz Trefftz (1926)

trial functions satisfy the PDE \[ \Delta u_h = 0 \ \text{in } \Omega \]

solve \[ \int_{\partial\Omega} u_h \, \dn v_h \,ds = \int_{\partial\Omega} g \, \dn v_h \,ds \quad \forall v_h \in \mathbb{T}^p \]

$2p+1 = \mathcal{O}(p^{d-1})$ dofs per element

Energy bounds: $\ \Vert \nabla u_h^{\text{Ritz}} \Vert_{L^2(\Omega)} \ \ge\ \Vert \nabla u \Vert_{L^2(\Omega)} \ \ge\ \Vert \nabla u_h^{\text{Trefftz}} \Vert_{L^2(\Omega)}$

E. Trefftz, Ein Gegenstück zum Ritzschen Verfahren, In Proc. 2nd Int. Cong. Appl. Mech., pages 131–137, Zürich, 1926.

Motivation

Trefftz basis functions

Local Trefftz space for a differential operator $\mathcal{L}$ on an element $K$:

\[ \mathbb{T}^p(K) = \{ v \in V^p(K) : \mathcal{L} v = 0 \text{ in } K \} \]
  • no continuity across elements: couple them with a DG technique
  • Laplace $\ \mathcal{L} = -\Delta$, $\ V^p(K) = \mathbb{P}^p(K)$: harmonic polynomials \[ 1,\ x,\ y,\ x^2 - y^2,\ xy,\ \dots \]
  • Helmholtz $\ \mathcal{L} = -\Delta - \omega^2$: plane waves \[ e^{i \omega \bm{d}_j \cdot \bm{x}}, \quad \vert \bm{d}_j \vert = 1, \quad j = 1, \dots, 2p+1 \]
Issue
  • No closed-form solutions for variable coefficients, right-hand sides $f \neq 0$, …
    • quasi-Trefftz spaces Imbert-Gérard, Després (2014)
  • Plane waves are ill-conditioned
    • evanescent plane waves Parolin, Huybrechs, Moiola (2023)

Motivation

How can we realise a more general Trefftz space?

  • The embedded Trefftz DG (ETDG) method Lehrenfeld, Stocker (2023); Lederer, Lehrenfeld, Stocker, Voulis (2024) relaxes the Trefftz constraint: \[ \IT = \{ v \in \Vh : \Pi_K \mathcal{L} v = 0 \ \forall K \in \Th \} \]
  • Main Idea: express Trefftz basis $\{ \psi_j \}_{j=1}^M$ in terms of DG basis $\{ \varphi_i \}_{i=1}^N$ of $\Vh = \IP^p(\Th)$ via embedding $\ T \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times M}$ \[ \text{Solve } T^\top A T = T^{\top} f \]
  • Elementwise construction: compute with SVD \[ T\vert_K = \ker (W_K), \qquad (W_K)_{ij} = (\mathcal{L} \varphi_j, \mathcal{L} \varphi_i)_K \]
  • Inhomogeneous problems $\mathcal{L} u = f$: compute particular solution with a pseudo-inverse, $\bm{u}_f\vert_K = W_K^\dagger \bm{w}_K$, $(w_K)_i = (f, \mathcal{L} \varphi_i)_K$, then $u_h = T \bm{u}_T + \bm{u}_f$
  • $\kappa(\Am^{\text{ETDG}}) \le \kappa(\Am^{\text{DG}})$

Motivation

Least Squares FEM

Least squares finite element methods (LSFEM) minimise the PDE residual Bochev, Gunzburger (2009)

\[ \A u = f \ \text{in } \Omega, \quad u = 0 \ \text{on } \partial \Omega \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad \begin{aligned} u &= \operatorname*{argmin}_{v \in X} \J(v), \qquad \J(v) = \Vert f - \A v \Vert^2_{L^2(\Omega)} \end{aligned} \]
  • symmetric positive definite
  • no inf-sup condition
  • built-in error estimator

1 Easily assembled

system matrix and right-hand side “easy”

✓ standard finite element integrals

2 Standard spaces

spaces no harder than for Galerkin methods

✗ needs $H^2$-conforming space

3 Conditioning

condition number as for Galerkin methods

✗ condition number $\mathcal{O}(h^{-4})$

  • First order system least squares (FOSLS): auxiliary $\bm{\sigma}$, mixed problem
    • less conformity, condition number $\mathcal{O}(h^{-2})$, but more degrees of freedom

The method

Application to Least Squares FEM

  • DG least squares (DGLS): find $u_h \in V_h \coloneqq \IP^p(\Th)$ such that $u_h$ solves \[ \min_{v_h \in \Vh} \J_h(v_h), \qquad \J_h(v_h) = \Vert f - \A v_h \Vert^2_{L^2(\Th)} + \vert v_h \vert^2_{j,h} \]
  • Conformity is replaced by a jump stabilisation: for $\alpha_d, \alpha_n, \alpha_b > 0$ \[ j_h(u,v) \coloneqq \sum_{F \in \Fhi} \left( \alpha_d \bigl( \dgjump{u}, \dgjump{v} \bigr)_{F} + \alpha_n \bigl( \dgjump{\nabla u \cdot \bm{n}}, \dgjump{\nabla v \cdot \bm{n}} \bigr)_{F} \right) + \sum_{F \in \Fhb} \alpha_b \bigl( u, v \bigr)_{F}, \qquad \vert v \vert^2_{j,h} \coloneqq j_h(v,v) \]
Embedded Trefftz DG least squares (ETLS)
Let $\Pi_K : \IP^p(K) \to \IP^{p-2}(K)$ be the elementwise $L^2$-orthogonal projection. We define the solution manifold \[ V_h^f \coloneqq \{ v_h \in \Vh : \Pi (\A v_h - f) = 0 \} = u_p + \IT. \] The ETLS method then solves the constrained minimisation problem \[ \min_{v_h \in V_h^f} \vert v_h \vert^2_{j,h}. \]

The method

Equivalent mixed formulation

Theorem (Equivalence)
Let $V_h^f \neq \emptyset$ and suppose that $\vert \cdot \vert_{j,h}$ is a norm on $\IT$. Then, the ETLS method has a unique solution $u_h \in V_h^f$ and the following are equivalent:
  1. $u_h$ solves the constrained minimisation problem \[ u_h \in \arg\min_{v_h \in V_h^f} \vert v_h \vert^2_{j,h}; \]
  2. $u_h$ solves the mixed problem: find $u_h \in \Vh$ such that \[ \begin{aligned} (\Pi \A u_h, q_h)_{L^2(\Th)} &= (\Pi f, q_h)_{L^2(\Th)} && \forall\, q_h \in \IP^{p-2}(\Th), \\ j_h(u_h, v_\IT) &= 0 && \forall\, v_\IT \in \IT; \end{aligned} \]
  3. $u_h = u_p + u_{\IT}$ with $u_{\IT} \in \IT$ solving $j_h(u_{\IT}, v_\IT) = - j_h(u_p, v_\IT)$ for all $v_\IT \in \IT$.

The method

Model problem

General model problem: find $u \in X$ such that \[ \A u \coloneqq \mathbb{A} : \hessian u + \mathbf{b} \cdot \nabla u + c u = f \]

where $\mathbb{A} \in L^\infty(\Omega, \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}_{\text{sym}})$, $\mathbf{b} \in L^\infty(\Omega, \mathbb{R}^d)$, and $c \in L^\infty(\Omega)$, with $\mathbb{A}$ uniformly elliptic, i.e., \[ \exists \alpha > 0 : \xi^\top \mathbb{A}(x) \xi \ge \alpha \vert \xi \vert^2 \quad \forall x \in \Omega, \quad \forall \xi \in \mathbb{R}^d. \]

  • Assumptions: $\Omega$ is sufficiently nice (e.g. a convex polytope) and $\A : H^2(\Omega) \cap H^1_0(\Omega) \to L^2(\Omega)$ is a bounded isomorphism; the DGLS method is stable on $V_h$
  • ETLS and DGLS norms on $\Vh$: \[ \Vert v_h \Vert^2_{\mathrm{ETLS},h} \coloneqq \Vert \Pi \A v_h \Vert^2_{L^2(\Th)} + \vert v_{\IT} \vert^2_{j,h}, \qquad \Vert v_h \Vert^2_{\mathrm{DGLS},h} \coloneqq \Vert \A v_h \Vert^2_{L^2(\Th)} + \vert v_h \vert^2_{j,h} \]
Theorem (stability and quasi-optimality)

For $h$ sufficiently small and $\mathbb{A}$ sufficiently nice, $\vert \cdot \vert_{j,h}^2$ is a norm on $\IT$ and $V_h^f \neq \emptyset$. Further

  1. the ETLS method has a unique solution $u_h \in V_h$
  2. norm equivalence: $C_1 \Vert v_h \Vert_{\mathrm{ETLS},h} \le \Vert v_h \Vert_{\mathrm{DGLS},h} \le C_2 \Vert v_h \Vert_{\mathrm{ETLS},h}$ for all $v_h \in \Vh$
  3. quasi-optimality: $\Vert u - u_h \Vert_{\mathrm{DGLS},h} \le C \inf_{v_h \in V_h} \Vert u - v_h \Vert_{\mathrm{DGLS},h}$

Analysis

A closer look at the assumptions

  • Weights in the seminorm: chosen to fit $H^2$-trace inequalities \[ j_h(v_h, v_h) = \sum_{F \in \Fhi} \left( h_F^{-3} \Vert \dgjump{v_h} \Vert^2_{L^2(F)} + h_F^{-1} \Vert \dgjump{\nabla v_h \cdot \bm{n}} \Vert^2_{L^2(F)} \right) + \sum_{F \in \Fhb} h_F^{-3} \Vert v_h \Vert^2_{L^2(F)}. \]
  • Assumptions on $\mathbb{A}$:
    1. Local Cordes condition (not too anisotropic): $\exists \gamma \in L^\infty(\Omega)$, $\gamma > 0$ such that \[ \delta_K \coloneqq \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{x \in K} \, \bigl\vert \gamma(x) \mathbb{A}(x) - \Idm \bigr\vert_F < 1\]
    2. Controlled oscillations (not too rough inside elements): \[ \operatorname{osc}_K(\gamma \mathbb{A}) \coloneqq \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{x \in K} \, \bigl\vert \gamma(x) \mathbb{A}(x) - \overline{\gamma(x) \mathbb{A}(x)} \bigr\vert_F < C (1- \delta_K)\]
  • Smallness of $h$: \[ C(1-\delta_K) - \operatorname{osc}_K(\gamma \mathbb{A}) - K \Vert \gamma \bm{b} \Vert_{L^\infty(K)} - h_K^2 \Vert \gamma c \Vert_{L^\infty(K)} > 0 \]

Analysis

Error estimator

We consider the elementwise estimator \[ \begin{aligned} \eta^2_K \coloneqq{}& \Vert f - \A u_h \Vert^2_{L^2(K)} + \frac{1}{2} \sum_{F \in \Fhi \cap \partial K} \Bigl( \alpha_d \Vert \dgjump{u_h} \Vert^2_{L^2(F)} + \alpha_n \Vert \dgjump{\nabla u_h \cdot \bm{n}} \Vert^2_{L^2(F)} \Bigr) + \sum_{F \in \Fhb \cap \partial K} \alpha_b \Vert u_h \Vert^2_{L^2(F)}, \qquad \eta^2 \coloneqq \sum_{K \in \Th} \eta^2_K \end{aligned} \]

  • $\eta$ is reliable and efficient with constant $1$ in the DGLS norm: for $u \in H^2(\Omega)$, \[ \eta = \Vert u - u_h \Vert_{\mathrm{DGLS},h} \]
  • We have that \[ \eta(u_h) \le C \eta(u_h^{\text{DG}})\le C \inf_{v_h \in V_h} \eta(v_h).\]

Numerical results

Basic validation: The Laplace problem

We consider the Laplace equation with Dirichlet boundary conditions:

\[ - \Delta u = f \text{ in } \Omega, \qquad u = g \text{ on } \Gamma_{\text{D}} \]
  • $\Omega = (0,1)^2$, $u_{\text{ex}} = e^x \sin y$, source $f = 0$, Dirichlet data from $u_{\text{ex}}$, $p = 2,\dots,5$
  • FOSLS as comparison: $\bm{\sigma} = \nabla u$ on the conforming pair $U_h = \IP^p(\Th) \cap H^1(\Omega)$ and $\Sigma_h \subset H(\div;\Omega)$ of order $p-1$: \[ \min_{(v, \bm{\tau}) \in U_h \times \Sigma_h} \Vert \div \bm{\tau} + f \Vert^2_{L^2(\Omega)} + \Vert \bm{\tau} - \nabla v \Vert^2_{L^2(\Omega)} + \alpha \Vert v -g \Vert^2_{L^2(\partial \Omega)} \]

Model problem

The Helmholtz equation

We consider the Helmholtz equation with mixed impedance and sound-soft boundary conditions:

\[ - \Delta u - \omega^2 u = f \text{ in } \Omega, \qquad \nabla u \cdot \bm{n} - i \omega u = 0 \text{ on } \Gamma_{\text{R}}, \qquad u = 0 \text{ on } \Gamma_{\text{D}} \]
  • Adaptivity: incoming plane wave scattered by an L-shaped sound-soft obstacle cf. Bespalov et al. (2019)
    • $\Omega = [-1,1]^2 \setminus L$, $\ \Gamma_{\text{D}} = \partial L$, $\ \Gamma_{\text{R}} = \partial \Omega \setminus \Gamma_{\text{D}}$
    • $\omega=65$, $p = 7$
    • Dörfler marking with $\theta_{\text{ref}} = 0.25$
Issue
  • Solution is not in $H^2(\Omega)$, minimising $\vert v_h \vert^2_{j,h,H^2}$ converges to the wrong solution.
  • The kernel space $\IT$ doesn't care \[ \{ (\sigma, u) : \sigma - \nabla u = 0, \Pi (\div \sigma + \omega^2 u + f ) = 0 \} = \{ (\nabla v, v) : \Pi( \Delta v + \omega^2 v + f ) = 0 \} \]
  • Can we adjust the minimisation problem? \[ \min_{v_h \in V_h^f} \vert v_h \vert^2_{j,h,H^2} \rightsquigarrow \min_{v_h \in V_h^f} \vert v_h \vert^2_{j,h,H^1} \]

Numerical results

Non-divergence form in 3D

We consider a non-divergence form problem \[ \Am(x) : \hessian u = f \text{ in } \Omega, \qquad u = g \text{ on } \partial \Omega, \] where $\Am$ is not differentiable, but fulfils a Cordes condition Smears, Süli (2013).
  • Example: $\Omega = (0,1)^3$, exact solution with point singularity \[ \Am(\bm{x}) = \Idm + \frac{\bm{x} \bm{x}^{\top}}{\vert \bm{x} \vert^2}, \qquad u_{\text{ex}} = \vert \bm{x} \vert^{1.6} \]
  • Linear system is SPD: solve with CG and block Jacobi
  • Adaptive Loop: Estimate with $\eta$ and use Dörfler marking with $\theta = 0.3$
  • Exponential convergence with hp-adaptivity

Extension to nonlinear problems

Trefftz for nonlinear problems

  • How can we treat nonlinear $\mathcal{F}(u) = f$?
  • Linearise (Newton, Picard): in step $k$ solve the linear problem \[ \A_k u^{k+1} = f_k, \qquad \A_k = D\mathcal{F}(u^k) \ \text{(or a frozen-coefficient operator)} \]
    • a new embedded Trefftz space $\IT_k = \{ v_h \in \Vh : \Pi_K \A_k v_h = 0 \ \forall K \in \Th \}$ in every step, then one ETLS solve Stocker, Voulis, Lehrenfeld, Lederer (2026)
Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equation with Cordes coefficients

$\Omega$ convex, $\Lambda$ compact metric space. Find $u$ with $u = 0$ on $\partial \Omega$ and \[ F[u] \coloneqq \sup_{\alpha \in \Lambda} \bigl( L^\alpha u - f^\alpha \bigr) = 0, \qquad L^\alpha v = a^\alpha : \hessian v + b^\alpha \cdot \nabla v - c^\alpha v, \] where the coefficients satisfy the Cordes condition Smears, Süli (2014)

  • Newton (Howard) step: choose $\alpha_k \in \arg\max_\alpha \bigl( L^\alpha u^k - f^\alpha \bigr)$ pointwise, then solve the linear non-divergence problem $L^{\alpha_k} u^{k+1} = f^{\alpha_k}$ in the Trefftz space of $L^{\alpha_k}$

Extension to nonlinear problems

Numerical example

Example 1 of Smears, Süli (2014): $\Omega = (0,1)^2$, $\Lambda = [0, \pi/3] \times SO(2)$, $b^\alpha = 0$, $c^\alpha = \pi^2$, \[ a^\alpha = \tfrac12 \sigma^\alpha (\sigma^\alpha)^\top, \qquad \sigma^\alpha = R^\top \begin{pmatrix} 1 & \sin\theta \\ 0 & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}, \qquad u_{\text{ex}} = e^{xy} \sin(\pi x) \sin(\pi y) \]

  • Uniform triangle meshes, $p = 2, \dots, 5$, Newton started from $u^0 = 0$
  • Optimal rate $\mathcal{O}(h^{p-1})$ in the broken $H^2$ norm
  • Damped Newton converges in $4$–$16$ steps, each with an SPD system
  • Fewer dofs: $2p+1$ instead of $\tfrac{(p+1)(p+2)}{2}$ per element

Conclusion

Embedded Trefftz least squares

Minimisation over $V_h^f = \{ v_h \in V_h: \Pi(\A v_h - f) = 0 \} = u_p + \IT$

\[ \min_{v_h \in \Vh} \vert v_h \vert_{j,h}^2 \ \text{ s.t. } \ \Pi(\A v_h - f) = 0 \qquad \Longleftrightarrow \qquad \min_{v_h \in u_p + \IT} \vert v_h \vert^2_{j,h} \]

1 Easily assembled

✓ usual DG integrals, small elementwise SVD

2 Standard spaces

✓ standard DG space $\IP^p(\Th)$, no conformity and no auxiliary variable

3 Conditioning

✗ $\mathcal{O}(h^{-4})$ inherited from the ambient DG space

What the method buys
  • Fewer dofs at the same accuracy, in particular for high $p$
  • Adaptivity: $\eta$ is a reliable and efficient estimator
  • Flexibility: variable and anisotropic $\Am$, non-divergence form, nonlinear $\A$, polytopal elements
